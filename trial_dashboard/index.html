      How these numbers are made

      Definitions

      LGU suspension: one city, town or province suspending classes for one day. School days lost: school days on which at least one LGU in the area suspended classes; a province counts a day even if only some of its towns suspended.

      School days: Monday to Friday throughout the year, minus national holidays (). Local holidays such as city fiestas aren't subtracted.

      Sources and limits

      Past days come from Rappler's daily #WalangPasok roundups, checked against the official PSGC list of places. On each day Rappler lists suspensions, PNA, DepEd and GMA were also searched (AI finds the articles and quotes them), adding places Rappler missed and confirming the ones it had. Every other week got a lighter search of the same outlets, which found more than 100 days with suspensions but no Rappler roundup (mostly earthquakes, heat, floods and transport strikes). A single school or university suspending counts as "some schools", not its whole town or province. Smaller suspensions can still be missed, so the counts are a lower bound. Coming days are checked every morning, or on demand, from PNA, DepEd and GMA (read by AI, which quotes the article) and Rappler (read without AI, as Rappler asks).

      Confidence (1–5): 5 = three or more outlets, or PNA/DepEd plus another; 4 = two outlets, or PNA/DepEd alone; 3 = one outlet; one point less for each missing detail. Past places found only by Rappler score 3 at most.

      Map: province boundaries from geoBoundaries (NAMRIA, PSA, OCHA; CC BY 3.0 IGO) for Luzon and the Visayas, and philippines-json-maps (PSA 2023; MIT) for Mindanao, which has the newer provinces.
