      How these numbers are made

      Definitions

      LGU suspension: one city, town or province suspending classes for one day. School days lost: school days on which at least one LGU in the area suspended classes; a province counts a day even if only some of its towns suspended.

      School days: every day in the period minus weekends, DepEd school breaks and national holidays (). Local holidays and private-school calendars can differ, so the percentage is an estimate.

      Sources and limits

      Past days come from Rappler's daily #WalangPasok roundups, checked against the official PSGC list of places. Rappler's lists miss many single-town suspensions, so the counts are a lower bound. Coming days are checked every morning, or on demand, from PNA, DepEd and GMA (read by AI, which quotes the article) and Rappler (read without AI, as Rappler asks).

      Confidence (1–5): 5 = three or more outlets, or PNA/DepEd plus another; 4 = two outlets, or PNA/DepEd alone; 3 = one outlet; one point less for each missing detail. Past days come from Rappler alone, so they score 3 at most.
