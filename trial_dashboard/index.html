    How these numbers are made

    Past suspensions

    Every place listed in Rappler's daily #WalangPasok roundups, read automatically and checked against the official PSGC list of provinces, cities and towns. The cause of each day comes from the roundup's own opening line (for example “due to Tropical Storm Enteng”).

    LGU suspension = one city, town or province suspending classes for one day. School days with suspensions = calendar days on which at least one LGU in the selected area did. A province's count includes days when only some of its towns suspended.

    Limits

    These are a lower bound. Rappler compiles roundups on days with widespread suspensions, so single-town suspensions (floods, bomb threats, local heat) are often missing: in a July 2024 test, reading more outlets found about 50% more suspended places than Rappler's lists alone. The past data will be rebuilt from all the major outlets below.

    “Face-to-face only” suspensions moved classes to modular or online learning instead of cancelling them.

    Upcoming suspensions

    Checked every day at 5 AM, and whenever someone presses Check now, from the major outlets: PNA, DepEd and GMA (read by AI, which quotes the article for every place it lists) and Rappler's daily roundup (read without AI, as Rappler asks). Inquirer blocks automated reading of its articles, so it isn't included yet. “The season ahead” is the 2025 average for each month, not a forecast.
